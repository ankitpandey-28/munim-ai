| D-11 | 2026-11-19 | Goods-receipt reversal valuation (Q-24) | Reversing a receipt backs its cost out of the weighted average: `new_avg = round_half_up((on_hand × avg − qty × receipt_cost) ÷ (on_hand − qty))`; avg kept if the remaining quantity is 0; result floored at 0. The reversal is **blocked** if it would make stock negative (goods already sold): correct with a stock count instead | Restores the pre-receipt valuation exactly in the common case; never invents negative stock | Stakeholder answer 2026-11-19 |
| D-12 | 2026-11-19 | Inventory rules (implementation, within approved policy) | (a) Opening stock with a unit cost enters the weighted average like a receipt; without a cost the average is unchanged. (b) Damage/expiry/loss adjustments cannot take stock below zero (no override exists for adjustments; use a count). (c) A stock count item stores the system quantity when counted; posting is rejected (`COUNT_STALE`) if stock moved since, so a difference is never applied twice. (d) Adjustment and count movements and sale-type outflows carry the current avg cost snapshot; reversing an outflow restores quantity at that snapshot and leaves the average unchanged. (e) Staff may view quantities and receive goods (SRS §3.2 default) but never see costs, averages or stock value | Conservative readings of SRS FR-INV-01..06, BR-STK-01..03 and database-design §4.3/S5; no new business policy | Derived; reviewable |
